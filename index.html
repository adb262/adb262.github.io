Writing

  1. May 25, 2026

    Unlabelled Gameplay as Pretraining for World Models

    The goal of this project was to see how much of a playable world model we could learn from video alone. Following Genie, we infer a latent action space directly from frame-to-frame change, using ground truth controls only for a small alignment step at the end. If it works, internet-scale gameplay video becomes training data. However, the industry has pulled away from this, often relying on game engines or video generation models to generate labelled trajectories. Why is that?
